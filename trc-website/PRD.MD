# THE RIDES CLUB

> **Ride it. Map it. Share it.**

---

## 01 — THE IDEA

The Rides Club is a home for motorcycle riders.

You ride somewhere.

You discover a road.

You find a café you've never seen before.

You stop at a viewpoint.

You take a photo.

And instead of that experience disappearing into your camera roll or a group chat, **The Rides Club turns it into something you can keep, share, and discover again.**

Think:

**Strava × Google Maps × a motorcycle community.**

But built specifically around the culture of riding.

---

# 02 — THE FEELING

The website should make someone who rides a motorcycle think:

> **"This was made for people like me."**

It shouldn't feel like a corporate SaaS landing page.

It should feel like:

**Friday night.
Helmet on.
Engine starts.
You have nowhere to be.**

The website sells the **feeling of riding**, not just a list of features.

---

# 03 — THE STORY

The website should tell a simple story as the visitor scrolls.

### YOU RIDE.

Show the motorcycle.

Show the road.

Show the beginning of a journey.

↓

### YOU EXPLORE.

Show the map.

Show routes.

Show destinations.

Show places worth stopping for.

↓

### YOU SHARE.

Show the ride.

Show the photos.

Show the route.

Show the experience.

↓

### YOU DISCOVER MORE.

Show other riders.

Show their routes.

Show places you've never been.

↓

### WELCOME TO THE RIDES CLUB.

Finish with the invitation to join.

---

# 04 — THE HERO

The first screen needs to communicate the entire idea almost instantly.

The visitor should understand:

**Motorcycles.
Rides.
Maps.
Community.**

The hero should have a strong statement and an obvious action.

Possible creative directions:

> **YOUR NEXT RIDE STARTS HERE.**

> **EVERY ROAD HAS A STORY.**

> **RIDE. EXPLORE. SHARE.**

> **WHERE RIDERS GO.**

These are creative directions, not final copy.

The final headline should be developed during design.

Primary CTA:

**Download the App**

If the app isn't available yet:

**Get Early Access**

---

# 05 — THE MAP IS THE STAR

The map shouldn't feel like an ordinary feature.

It should feel like **the world of The Rides Club.**

Imagine opening the website and seeing roads covered with stories:

```text
                    🏍️

              ╱────────────╲
             ╱              ╲
        📍───╯                ╰──📍
       Café                    Viewpoint
          ╲                      ╱
           ╲────── 🏍️ ─────────╯

                 87 KM
```

Routes become experiences.

Pins become destinations.

The map becomes a visual representation of the community.

---

# 06 — THE THREE HERO FEATURES

Don't overwhelm people with ten feature cards.

Lead with three ideas.

## 🏍️ TRACK YOUR RIDE

Start riding.

The Rides Club records the journey.

Distance.
Duration.
Route.
Elevation.

Your ride becomes a story on the map.

---

## 🗺️ DISCOVER THE ROAD

Find places other riders have discovered.

Scenic roads.

Cafés.

Viewpoints.

Beaches.

Hidden stops.

Your next destination might already be waiting on the map.

---

## 📸 SHARE THE JOURNEY

Your ride doesn't end when the engine stops.

Share the route.

Drop the photos.

Tell the story.

Let other riders see where you've been.

---

---

# 08 — THE WORLD OF RIDERS

The Rides Club isn't just a tracker.

It's a community.

The website should show that every ride contributes to a larger world of riders.

One rider discovers a road.

Another rider discovers a café.

Someone else takes the same route a month later.

The map becomes a collection of experiences.

---

# 09 — THE PRODUCT PREVIEW

Show the app rather than explaining it endlessly.

Use:

* Phone mockups
* App screens
* Map views
* Ride cards
* Profile screens
* Destination pages

The visitor should be able to **see themselves using it.**

If final app screens don't exist yet, use intentional concept UI.

Never present fictional functionality as an existing production feature.

---

# 10 — THE CTA

The final section should feel like an invitation rather than a sales pitch.

Something along the lines of:

> **THE ROAD IS WAITING.**

or

> **READY TO RIDE?**

Then:

**Join The Rides Club.**

[ Download the App ]

The exact copy will be determined during the design process.

---

# 11 — VISUAL DIRECTION

The website should use the project's custom:

**`funky-bubble-design`**

skill.

That skill controls the visual language.

The PRD does not override it.

The design should make The Rides Club recognizable and memorable while still keeping the motorcycle/adventure subject matter clear.

The website should feel like a **brand**, not a template.

---

# 12 — THE EXPERIENCE

The ideal scroll should feel like a ride:

```text
START
  ↓
"What's this?"
  ↓
"Oh, it's for riders."
  ↓
"That's a cool map."
  ↓
"I can track my rides?"
  ↓
"I can discover places?"
  ↓
"Other riders are here too."
  ↓
"I want this."
  ↓
JOIN THE RIDES CLUB
```

The page should progressively reveal the product instead of dumping every feature onto the visitor.

---

# 13 — MVP

The first version only needs to nail the story.

### Must have

* Hero
* Product introduction
* Ride tracking showcase
* Interactive/map visual
* Ride sharing showcase
* Place discovery showcase
* App preview
* Community section
* Download / early-access CTA
* FAQ
* Footer
* Responsive mobile experience

### Nice to have

* Scroll-driven map animation
* Interactive route visualization
* Animated ride paths
* Micro-interactions
* Dynamic ride cards
* Experimental transitions

Don't let the "nice to have" features delay the core experience.

---

# 14 — WHAT WE ARE NOT BUILDING

This repository is **the website.**

Not the app.

Don't build:

* GPS tracking
* Real-time ride recording
* Mobile authentication
* App backend
* Social feed backend
* Ride database
* Push notifications
* Production ride synchronization

The website can **show** these experiences.

It doesn't need to **implement** them.

---

# 15 — THE NORTH STAR

When making a design or product decision, ask:

> **Would a motorcycle rider want to stop scrolling and see where this road leads?**

If yes:

**keep going.**

If no:

**make it better.**
